FAQ

Quick answers before download.

Does the user download latest.yml?

No. Forge reads latest.yml from the release in the background. Users download the setup or zip.

What should a release include?

Upload the setup exe, matching blockmap, latest.yml, and the public Forge zip for the website.

Why does internal version matter?

Electron compares semver. The updater version must be higher than the installed version even if the public name is different.
